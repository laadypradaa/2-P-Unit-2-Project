👸🏽Princess Tiana👸🏽

Tiana’s Palace is a vibrant, New Orleans-inspired eatery that originated as the central dream in Disney’s 2009 animated film The Princess and the Frog, before opening as a real quick-service restaurant at Disneyland’s New Orleans Square on September 7, 2023.

Background💚

The restaurant represents the culmination of a lifelong dream shared by Tiana and her late father, James. Through years of hard work, saving every dime from multiple waitressing jobs, and surviving a chaotic adventure in the bayou, Tiana finally purchases a rundown, abandoned sugar mill. With the help of her friends and family, she transforms the decrepit building into the city's premier jazz club and restaurant, running it alongside her husband, Prince Naveen, and their trumpet-playing alligator friend, Louis.

Character⭐

The character of Tiana herself along with her passion for feeding people was heavily inspired by the late Leah Chase, a legendary New Orleans chef and civil rights pioneer who ran the famous Dooky Chase’s Restaurant. Furthermore, when Disney animators were originally designing the fictional restaurant for the 2009 movie, they modeled its look directly after Disneyland's historic French Market Restaurant.
